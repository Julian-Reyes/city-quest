# Quests: what to do next

Status: **future product direction — not implemented**.

## Agreed direction

Give Quests a forward-looking purpose: help users choose their next adventure.
The user prefers this direction over Quests primarily summarizing past visits.

The three screens should serve distinct purposes:

- **Quests — what to do next:** actionable goals, progress toward completion,
  and a clear next step.
- **Passport — where you've been:** a personal collection of places visited,
  stamps, photos, notes, and visit history.
- **Achievements — milestones you've earned:** recognition for exploration
  and activity over time.

## Why change Quests?

The current Quests screen summarizes saved discoveries by quest category and
city. Passport already shows those discoveries as individual places, so the
screens overlap. City and category counts alone give users little reason to
open a separate Quests screen.

## Future Quests experience

Use the existing Food & Drink, Day Explorer, Culture Run, and Active categories
to organize goals. Each quest should make these points clear:

- What the user can do next.
- How much progress they have made toward a specific goal.
- Which action or place could help them continue.
- When the quest is complete.

Example concept: **Visit three cafés in San Francisco.** Show progress toward
that goal and a useful way to find the next café. This is an illustrative
example, not a finalized quest definition.

Keep city context useful for choosing local adventures. Decide which goals
belong to a specific city and which work globally, rather than automatically
duplicating every goal across both scopes.

## Decisions for a later implementation task

- Choose an initial set of goals and their completion criteria.
- Decide whether past visits count or progress starts when a quest is selected.
- Define whether progress counts distinct places, repeat visits, or other
  activities; make that rule clear to users.
- Decide whether quests are always available or must be started, and how
  completed quests are displayed.
- Connect progress to real saved visits and provide an actionable next step.
- Decide whether rewards are needed; do not invent XP, levels, or streaks
  without a separate product decision.
- Consider moving the existing category summaries into Passport so they remain
  available without being the main purpose of Quests.

This document records direction for future work. It does not implement quests,
change the UI, or finalize the open decisions above.
